How I teach

Most adult learners do not need more grammar rules. They need someone to listen closely, catch the same six or seven mistakes they always make, and help them stop making them.

So my lessons start from you. You speak and write about things that matter in your life: your work, your exam, your next interview. I note what goes wrong, show you the correct version and explain the rule in plain words. After the lesson you get the corrections in writing.

Over a few weeks the list of repeated mistakes gets shorter. That is the progress you can feel, and the kind an examiner or a colleague notices.

Who I teach

My students are mostly adults in Europe. Many work in fields such as logistics, healthcare, marketing and engineering, and need English for meetings, emails and interviews. Others are preparing for IELTS, ECL or another exam with a score to reach by a fixed date.

Outside lessons

Alongside tutoring I work in commercial estimating and project management for a UK company. It keeps my business English current, because I write the same emails and sit in the same meetings my students are preparing for.
