What I collect when you book a call or buy lessons, why, and what you can ask me to do with it.

What I collect

  • Your name and email address, when you book a call or a lesson.
  • What you tell me about your goals, level and deadlines, in the booking form, by email or in lessons.
  • Work you send me to correct, such as essays, emails or slides.
  • A record of your purchases. Card payments are handled by Stripe; I never see your full card details.

Why I use it

To arrange and teach your lessons, send you corrections and lesson materials, answer your messages, and keep the financial records the law requires. I do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Services I use

Bookings and video lessons run on Google Calendar and Google Meet, email on Google Workspace, and payments on Stripe. These companies process your information on my behalf and may store it outside the UK, with legal safeguards in place.

How long I keep it

Lesson notes and correspondence are kept while you are a student and for up to two years afterwards, then deleted. Purchase records are kept for six years for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it where I no longer need it. Email {{email}}. If you are unhappy with how I handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
