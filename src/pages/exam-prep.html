How the course is built

ECL tests reading, writing, listening and speaking. The course prepares each one, in an order that lets vocabulary carry over from paper to paper.

  • Topics in groups

    The exam's long topic list is sorted into a small number of groups. You learn the language for one group at a time and reuse it in writing and speaking that week.

  • A vocabulary bank per group

    For each topic group you get the words, collocations and phrases a C1 candidate is expected to use, ready to revise from.

  • Mocks marked to the criteria

    You sit mock writing and speaking tests. I mark them the way the exam is marked and show you which criteria are costing you points.

  • A plan for each week

    Five weeks, two lessons a week, with set work in between. You always know what is due and why.

I also prepare students for other C1 and B2 English exams using the same method: your writing marked to that exam's criteria, speaking mocks, and a weekly plan. Tell me which exam and when, and I will say whether I am the right tutor for it.
