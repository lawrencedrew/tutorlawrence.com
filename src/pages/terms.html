Moving or cancelling a lesson

You can move or cancel a lesson free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice, using the link in your calendar invitation. A lesson cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed, counts as taken.

Your right to cancel

If you book and take lessons within those 14 days, you are asking me to start before the cancellation period ends. If you then cancel within the 14 days, you receive a refund minus the price of the lessons already taken, worked out as an equal share of the package price.

After 14 days, payments for packages are not refundable, except for lessons I cannot provide. This does not affect your legal rights.
