Questions people ask

Which package is right for me?

If you have an exam date, choose the exam package. If English is slowing you down at work, choose the Business English programme. If you have an interview in the next two weeks, choose the interview intensive. If none fits, tell me on the call.

What happens if I need to move a lesson?

Tell me at least 24 hours before and we move it at no cost. A lesson cancelled with less notice counts as taken.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Email me the company details and I will send an invoice addressed to your employer instead of card payment.

What do I need for lessons?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, a quiet room and a stable connection. Headphones help.

Can I continue after a package ends?

Yes. At the final review we look at what is left to do and you can book another package if it is worth it.
